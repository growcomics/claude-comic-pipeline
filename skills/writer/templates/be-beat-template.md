# BE Beat-Sheet Template — breast expansion

*Type `be` (legacy Bloom type; `bg` is the canonical combined type) per `production-briefing/SKILL.md`. Lineup: `breast-size-lineup.png`. House conventions: hourglass maintained throughout (small waist, broad hips at every tier); round symmetric shape; no muscle-region beats unless the concept adds them.*

**Region menu**: `chest` (the spine of every run), `suit_fail`, `whole_body` — with `hips` only if the concept's flavor includes it. Do **not** reach for `arms`/`abs`/`back` beats; that's FMG grammar.

**Signature devices**: `clothing-destruction` as the *ambient* tell (buttons, zippers, stretched prints, gapping plackets — the continuous strain story between beats), `multi-panel-progressive` on the chest (the BE triptych), `sfx-driven` (creak → pop → rip at seams, never past coverage), `reaction-intercut` (BE lives on faces — hers and the witness's), `size-comparison` (armfuls, shelf-vs-torso, doorway turns).

**Emotional register**: playful overwhelm → delighted disbelief → proud display (`posing-and-expressions.md` Prompt 1's four reaction modes are the palette). Posture business is first-class: arched back, adjusted balance, arms framing or catching the new weight.

## Skeleton (N pages; growth floor by CHAPTER-TYPE)

```
ACT 1 — the want (pages 1 … ~0.15N)
  p1        PROMISE: an ordinary want the growth will answer sideways (be noticed,
            fill the dress, win the pageant slot, out-shine the rival).
            beats: consider → decide on the engine

SCENE 1 — first swell (by ~0.15N; 3+ growth panels; modest tier step)
  beats: trigger → first_sensation → chest → chest (progressive pair) → mini reveal
  devices: sfx-driven + reaction-intercut
  business: the top goes from loose to fitted to strained; first button gives
  face rule: chest ECUs interleave with her face — gasp, glance down, grin

MIDDLE — consequence + escalation
  wardrobe negotiation IS plot (the borrowed jacket, the re-tied wrap, the
  jacket that no longer closes). The rival/witness reacts; stakes move.
  optional SCENE 2: another chest run, one tier up, a new garment sacrificed,
  a new gauge (the doorway sideways-turn, the armful test).

SCENE FINAL — the big swell (~0.7–0.8N; 3+ growth panels; → declared end tier)
  beats: trigger → chest → suit_fail (the seam moment — coverage holds) →
         whole_body → reveal (grid-break splash: full figure, proud display,
         hourglass intact)
  devices: multi-panel-progressive + clothing-destruction + size-comparison
  staging: negative-space-asymmetric solo reveal, or depth-staged with the
           witness small in BG

LANDING
  aftermath: the want answered; posture proud, wardrobe visibly renegotiated,
  one consequence beat + closing line.
```

**Coverage is absolute**: strain, gap, split at seams — never past coverage of the chest; `ALWAYS-CLOTHED` holds at every tier. Size is never the SFW problem; exposure is.
