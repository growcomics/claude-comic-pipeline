# MMG Beat-Sheet Template — male muscle growth

*Type `mmg` per `production-briefing/SKILL.md` (MaxxMuscleComics / male 3DMC). Lineup: `male-muscle-lineup.png`. House conventions: male anatomy throughout — **never feminize the figure during growth** (no breast beats, no hourglass language); V-taper, trap/delt/neck mass, jaw/brow heavying are the silhouette tells.*

**Region menu**: `arms`, `chest`, `shoulders`, `back`, `abs`, `legs`, `suit_fail`, `whole_body`. (No `chest`-as-breasts grammar — chest = pecs; no `hips`/`rear` beats unless the concept explicitly wants lower-body mass.)

**Signature devices**: `sfx-driven` (seam-burst percussion — shirts die shoulder-first), `multi-panel-progressive` (bicep/back triptychs), `size-comparison` (the former bully, the doorway he now fills, the shirt that was baggy this morning), `full-body-reveal`, `reaction-intercut` (his own disbelief→confidence arc is the emotional spine; witnesses recoil or reappraise).

**Feat + dominance language**: every tier bump buys a feat, and the ladder runs against *people* — carried, out-lifted, loomed over, moved aside effortlessly. Confidence transformation parallels the physical one: posture, chin, voice. (Menace is allowed — SFW constrains coverage, not attitude.)

## Skeleton (N pages; growth floor by CHAPTER-TYPE)

```
ACT 1 — the want (pages 1 … ~0.15N)
  p1        PROMISE: ordinary want (respect, the spot, standing up to the rival,
            being seen). Establish him small against the gauge character.
            beats: consider → decide on the engine

SCENE 1 — first surge (by ~0.15N; 3+ growth panels; start→+1..2)
  beats: trigger → first_sensation → arms → shoulders/chest → mini reveal
  devices: sfx-driven + zoom-escalation
  business: sleeves fill then split at the seam; collar tightens; first feat
  face rule: intercut his stunned/grinning face between region crops

MIDDLE — consequence + escalation
  the world re-measures him: the rival's tone changes, the task that beat him
  yesterday is easy today. Optional SCENE 2: back/legs run, +1 tier, a bigger
  feat, a new gauge (he fills the doorframe; the machine maxes out).

SCENE FINAL — peak (~0.7–0.8N; → declared end tier)
  beats: trigger → chest → back → suit_fail (shirt dies across the shoulders) →
         abs/legs → whole_body → reveal (grid-break splash: low-angle hero
         full-body, V-taper dominant)
  devices: multi-panel-progressive + size-comparison + full-body-reveal
  staging: depth-staged (former tormentor small in BG) or worms-eye
           negative-space-asymmetric solo

LANDING
  aftermath: the want answered on HIS terms — respect given, rival deflated or
  converted; consequence beat + closing line.
```

**Coverage**: `ALWAYS-CLOTHED` — shirts shred at shoulders/sleeves and stay draped/hanging; waist coverage always preserved. Shirtless-by-destruction only if the user explicitly clears it — default keeps a ruined garment on the figure.
