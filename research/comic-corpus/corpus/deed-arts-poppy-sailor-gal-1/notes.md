## Growth-page accounting
- Growth pages (trigger/early/mid/peak OR role==active-growth), pages 1-17 only (p18 is the issue-2 cover, excluded): **p5, p6, p7, p8, p12, p13, p14, p15, p16, p17 = 10 / 17 → growth-page ratio = 59%**
- Transformation scenes:
  - **Poppy's spinach transformation — p5-8** — devices: multi-panel-progressive, clothing-destruction, zoom-escalation, size-comparison, sfx-driven, full-body-reveal. She drinks a glowing capsule found belowdecks, swells out of her uniform over 4 pages, ends in a nude full-body flex reveal (p8).
  - **Fight-and-fade coda — p9-10** — Poppy (now huge) beats the pirate crew, poses, then the power visibly wears off (p10n3, translucent afterimage + "SHRINK" sfx) — a genuine Popeye-style temporary-strength payoff, not a permanent transformation.
  - **Captain & crew's spinach transformation — p11-17** — devices: multi-panel-progressive, clothing-destruction, zoom-escalation, size-comparison, slow-burn, reaction-intercut, sfx-driven, full-body-reveal (8 devices — the strongest scene in the book). The defeated captain wakes, finds the spinach trail (p11), eats it herself (p12), grows through a body-part-ECU column (p13) and a 3-panel flex ladder (p14), the effect spreads to her whole crew (p15-16), and the issue closes on a full-bleed splash of the entire hyper-muscular crew rallying for revenge (p17).
- Zoom coverage: 32 of 79 panels (40.5%) are tagged `growth_focus`; 11 of those are ECU/CU tight body-part crops. Both arcs use stacked/sequential ECUs (p7: chest→chest→arms→glutes; p13: chest→chest→abs→glutes).

## Camera
- **Distance spread across the book:** strong overall (diagonal on 54/79 panels, scale-contrast on 32, depth-fg-bg on 22, low-hero on 20, foreshortening on 17). The most cinematically varied pages are the setup/confrontation pages (p1, p3, p5, p11 all distance_spread=4), which range EWS-aerial establishing shots down to ECU inset details in a single page.
- **Flat-panel hotspots:** only 6/79 panels are flagged `flat-level`, and most are defensible (map/prop insets with no figure: p1n3, p11n4; a plain establishing shot: p2n1). The two genuine flat-staging misses are **p3 panel 2** (a static reaction close-up on a shock beat that could have used an angle) and **p10 panel 2** (Poppy posing level-eyeline at the ship's wheel right after her most dynamic panel of the book, p10n1's dutch-tilt flex — a deliberate come-down beat, but it reads static).
- **Distance monotony (the real soft spot):** pages 6, 7, 8, 13, and 14 all hold to only 2 distinct shot_distances — the growth bursts trade shot-size variety for sustained zoom intensity. Effective as a stylistic choice (it reads as "the camera won't let go of the muscle"), but it's the reason camera dynamism scores 4 and not 5.
- **Best dynamic compositions to steal:** see the Angle & pose study section below — p8/p14's flex ladders, p7/p13's ECU columns, and p17's full-bleed crew splash are the standouts.

## Expressions
- **Where faces carry the beat:** both transformation arcs regularly hit peak intensity. Standouts: **p6n4** (whole-body trembling ecstasy as the first growth ends), **p8n4** ("still growing" joy-5 at the largest flex), **p12n4** (shocked wide-eyed reveal with a crushed prop in hand), **p13n2** (gritted-teeth strain-5 chest ECU), **p14n3-4** (smug-5 → ecstasy-5 across the flex ladder's last two panels), **p15n5-6** (matched shock-5 reactions as the growth trigger spreads to two crew members), and **p17n2** (joy-5 pride shot post-transformation).
- **Dead-face defects (the call-out):** 7 of 32 growth-focus panels go faceless — **p7 panels 2 & 4** (chest and glute ECUs), **p8 panel 1** (glute/thigh worm's-eye ECU), **p12 panel 5** (torso-only reveal, head cropped out), **p13 panels 3-4** (abs and glutes ECUs), **p14 panel 1** (shoulder-tear inset). This is a real but *contained* version of the "hero's own peak goes faceless" pattern seen elsewhere in the corpus — here the studio consistently brackets the faceless ECUs with high-intensity face panels immediately before/after (e.g. p13n2 face-5 leads directly into the faceless n3-4), so the gap is smaller but still worth flagging.
- One low-confidence read: **p16 panel 2** — the dialogue balloon ("CAPTAIN WHATS HAPPENING?!") reads as alarm, but the drawn face (tongue out, half-lidded eyes) reads as ecstasy; tagged to the drawn expression per the rubric, but it's a genuine mismatch worth a second look (see Uncertain pages below).

## Story & structure
- **Hook:** fast. By page 4 of 17 (~24% in) the protagonist has been established, humiliated by a rival, and is already flexing on a crewmate; the actual growth trigger lands on page 5.
- **Pacing:** a genuine two-act mirror — Act 1 (Poppy's temporary growth, p1-10) and Act 2 (the captain's crew permanent growth, p11-17) — which keeps the second half from feeling like a rerun of the first (unlike a straight one-and-done). The soft spot is **p9-11**: after Poppy's growth peaks on p8, the book spends a fight (p9), a fade-out (p10), and a slow re-discovery walk (p11) before Act 2's growth restarts on p12 — three pages of connective tissue right after the first payoff.
- **Tease vs payoff:** Act 1 is fast-payoff (trigger on p5, peak by p8, almost no anticipation built beforehand). Act 2 is a genuine slow burn — the captain wakes on p11, doesn't drink the spinach until p12, and the growth doesn't fully resolve until p17 — six pages of delay before release. Directly relevant reference point for the redhead-houseguest edging structure: this book demonstrates both modes back to back in the same issue, and the slow-burn version (Act 2) reads as the stronger of the two specifically because more page-time is spent on anticipation (p11's investigation, p15's "wakes the crew" trigger-spread) before the payoff.
- **Cliffhanger / continuity:** ends on a genuine pull-forward — the full-bleed splash on p17 shows the captain rallying her now-hyper-muscular crew for revenge; the issue-2 cover (p18) pays this off visually with the captain already transformed, standing alongside a still-normal-scale Poppy — a size-reversal hook for the sequel.
- **Dialogue & SFX:** dialogue is present and load-bearing throughout (unlike the corpus's other reviewed comic, which shipped every balloon empty) — it establishes the treasure macguffin, the rivalry, and the "spinach" gag directly. SFX are the workhorse for the growth beats specifically: BULGE/RIIIP/SWELL/BOING/STRETCHHHH chains (heaviest on p13-14 and p16-17) carry sensation even through the faceless ECUs noted above.
- **Clarity:** strong. The Popeye pastiche (spinach = strength) is instantly legible the moment it's introduced (p6n2's "LEGENDARY PIRATE'S STRENGTH" spinach cans), so the reader never has to infer the growth mechanic — a clean structural win the pipeline should note: a one-panel visual gag (spinach cans) does the explanatory work no dialogue block could do as efficiently.

**Narrative arc:** ordinary adventurer sails for legendary treasure → a rival pirate captain and crew steal it from her → she stumbles on glowing "spinach" capsules and temporarily grows into a muscle powerhouse → beats the crew, reclaims the treasure, and the power fades → the defeated captain finds the spinach trail and feeds it to herself and her crew → they permanently transform into a hyper-muscular gang and rally for revenge (to be continued).

## Angle & pose study

**Camera-to-body relationship.** This artist stages almost every muscle beat from **chest height or eye level looking either level or up** (`camera_height`: chest 36/79, eye 32/79 — combined 86% of all panels) — he essentially never shoots down at his own cast during a growth or flex beat; `above-head`/`top-down` appear only 5 times total, reserved for establishing shots or a single POV gag (p6n2). Low-hero framing (`angle: low` on 30/79 panels, plus a single worm's-eye and a single dutch tilt for the two most extreme beats) is the default "muscle is impressive" camera; level eye-angle (41/79) is reserved for dialogue and mid-growth reaction beats, not power poses.

**Which limb goes at the lens, and how often.** `toward_camera` is `chest` on 31/79 panels (39%) — by far the dominant choice; this artist sells growth chest-first more than any other body part. Forearm/fist/elbow (arm-terminus parts) combine for 12 more panels, and glutes get 4 dedicated close crops (all in the two growth scenes, never elsewhere) via `body_line: arch-back`/`coil-crouch` rear three-quarter poses. Back is thrust at the lens only twice, both late in the book (p16n4, p17n1) as the growth spreads to secondary crew — a device the artist reaches for only once he needs a *new* muscle group to keep the escalation from repeating itself.

**How he crops the figure.** `crop` skews toward `waist-up` and `chest-up` for flex poses (the double-biceps "flex ladder" is always cropped waist-up so both raised fists stay in frame) and `body-part-only` for the ECU growth beats (chest, glutes, shoulder). Full-figure `crop: full` is reserved for the two nude full-body reveals (p8n2-3) and the wide establishing/splash shots (p1n1, p17n3) — full-body crops are rare and specifically saved for "unveil the whole result" beats, not for in-progress growth.

**How he sells each muscle group** (`muscle_sold` histogram, figure-bearing panels only — 54 of 79 panels have a nonzero value): **arms 24, chest 16, full-silhouette 6, glutes 4, shoulders 2, abs 1, back 1**. Arms are sold almost entirely through `sell_mechanism: foreshortening` + repeated flex poses; chest through `cloth-strain` (garment tension/tearing) more than through nudity itself; glutes through extreme low camera height + `contact-deform` (fabric deforming around the mass); the single `back` and `abs` beats are late-book "keep it fresh" choices rather than a repeated device.

**Page-layout rhythm.** Growth pages consistently escalate panel *size* toward the bottom of the page — the biggest, most bled-out panel is reserved for the page's final beat (p6n4, p8n4, p12n4-5, p14n4, p17n3 are all the largest panel on their page). Panel *shape* also escalates: growth pages start with a tighter grid (2x2 on p6-7) and loosen into fewer, larger, diagonally-bordered panels as the sequence peaks (p13-14's four-panel pages abandon a strict grid entirely for tilted, overlapping borders).

**How he stages growth across panels.** Two repeatable templates: (1) the **body-part ECU column** — 3-4 sequential extreme close-ups on different muscle groups tied together by one continuous lightning/energy motif and escalating sfx stacks (p7, p13); (2) the **flex ladder** — the identical pose (front double-biceps) redrawn 3 times at increasing panel scale down the page, the pose itself never changing, only the size and the sfx count (p8, p14). Both templates end on a full-width/full-bleed panel that is explicitly the largest on the page.

## What does NOT translate to 3D
- **Stacked repeated onomatopoeia carrying a faceless/off-panel beat** (BULGE BULGE BULGE, SWELL SWELL SWELL — p13n3-4, p16n1-2) — no text layer in a photoreal render; the pose/tension/lighting must do this work instead.
- **Muscle mass literally breaking the panel's own border** (p8n4, p14n4: "muscles now crowd past the panel's own borders") — a 2D page-layout flourish with no single-camera equivalent; substitute an extreme frame-crop where the mass fills the lens.
- **Verbatim panel-to-panel pose repeats as the entire escalation mechanic** (p8n2-4, p14n2-4 redraw the identical double-bicep pose bigger each time) — cheap in ink where the linework alone reads as "bigger"; a CGI sequence needs real incremental pose/expression/tension change or it reads as a lazy re-render.
- **Grayscale/desaturation as a reveal device** (p2n4's silhouette-captain cliffhanger) — a stylistic ink trick; in a photoreal pipeline a sudden desaturated panel reads as a color-grade bug, not a deliberate reveal.
- **Nested, non-standard panel borders bleeding into an unbordered energy backdrop** (p3's layered insets floating on a borderless lightning-cracked background) — a page-layout composition device with no single-panel photoreal equivalent.

## Strengths to steal
- **Run two complete transformation arcs off one mechanic** instead of one-and-done: Poppy's temporary growth (p5-8, pays off and visibly fades by p10) and the captain's crew's permanent growth (p12-17, pays off in a cliffhanger) double the growth-page count without repeating the same beat.
- **The flex ladder** — identical pose, escalating panel scale, 3 repeats (p8n2-4; p14n2-4) — a dead-simple, highly legible escalation device.
- **The body-part ECU column** — p13's chest→chest→abs→glutes in 4 sequential tilted panels tied by one continuous lightning motif is a clean, reusable multi-panel-progressive template.
- **Dynamic camera bias isn't reserved for money shots** — even p1n4, a plain map-tearing beat with zero growth content, gets a low-angle chest-height bicep flex; only 6/79 panels (7.6%) are flat-level across the whole book.
- **SFX chains carry sensation through faceless/backgroundless panels** — BULGE/RIIIP/SWELL/BOING (heaviest p13-14, p16-17) keep the zoom-escalation device working even when a panel has no face or background.
- **Scale-contrast onlookers sell size cheaply** — tiny reacting pirates below Poppy's silhouette (p9n2-3) and a normal-scale captain flanked by hyper-muscular crew in the closing splash (p17n3) sell scale without a measured prop.
- **A one-panel visual gag can replace a dialogue block** — the spinach-can label (p6n2) explains the entire growth mechanic instantly; the pipeline should look for equivalent single-image shortcuts to exposition.

## Weaknesses to avoid
- **Faceless growth ECUs recur in both arcs** (p7n2 & n4, p8n1, p12n5, p13n3-4, p14n1) — 7 of 32 growth-focus panels (22%) lack a face; a cut-in reaction panel would close the gap cheaply (the book already does this well elsewhere — see p13n2, p14n3-4).
- **Pages 9-11 sag the pacing** — three pages of fight/fade/re-discovery connective tissue right after the first payoff (p8) cool the momentum before Act 2 restarts on p12.
- **Panel-boundary legibility gets shaky on the busiest layout pages** — p3's borderless energy backdrop with floating insets, p11's giant foreground object spanning two tiers, and p12's two growth beats merged into one wide panel were all flagged as genuine reading-order judgment calls during this analysis; a human pass should confirm intended order before this material is used as a generation reference.
- **The "muscle breaks the panel border" trick doesn't translate** (p8n4, p14n4) — needs a CGI substitute (extreme frame-crop) rather than literal imitation.
- **Distance monotony inside both growth bursts** — pages 6, 7, 8, 13, 14 each hold to only 2 distinct shot_distances; effective as a stylistic choice but it's the reason camera dynamism scores 4 and not 5.
