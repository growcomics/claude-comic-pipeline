**Does not translate to 3D CGI (Deed Arts):**
- **Impossible crossed-arm forearm interlock in the signature rear pose** — Reads fine as flat linework but the forearms overlap in ways a rigged 3D figure can't physically replicate; re-block the arm crossing for CGI. _(p1.1, p20.1, p21.1, p21.5)_
- **Claw-shaped radiating force lines drawn directly onto the muscle surface** — A 2D FX convention with no 3D equivalent; replace with practical rim light, particle dust, or a post-process glow instead of trying to model the lines. _(p4.1, p4.2, p4.3, p4.4)_
- **Solid-black cape/cloth tearing into hard graphic shapes** — Reads as fabric only through flat silhouette; needs an actual cloth simulation with real tear geometry to survive the move to 3D. _(p4.2)_
- **Stacked-ghost-repeat single-panel growth timeline** — A compositing trick, not a single-camera shot; a literal one-shot recreation isn't possible, it must be built as a multi-render composite. _(p3.1)_
- **Halo/glow rim outlining entire figures against the background** — A flat ink/shader trick; needs to become genuine rim/backlighting in the 3D render rather than a uniform outline. _(p0.1, p1.1, p3.1, p5.1, p10.1, p41.1)_
- Stacked repeated onomatopoeia (BULGE BULGE BULGE, SWELL SWELL SWELL) doing the primary work of selling an off-panel or faceless growth beat (p13 panels 3-4, p16 panels 1-2) -- a photoreal panel has no text layer to lean on; the pose, tension, and lighting have to carry what the sfx repetition carries here.
- Muscle mass literally breaking the panel's own border (p8 panel 4, p14 panel 4: 'muscles now crowd past the panel's own borders') -- a 2D page-layout trick with no single-camera equivalent; substitute an extreme frame-crop where the mass fills the lens instead.
- Verbatim panel-to-panel pose repeats used as the entire escalation mechanism (p8 panels 2-4, p14 panels 2-4 redraw the identical double-bicep pose bigger each time) -- cheap in ink where the linework alone reads as 'bigger'; a CGI render needs genuine incremental pose/expression/tension change or the sequence reads as a lazy re-render, not escalation.
- Grayscale/desaturation used as a reveal device (p2 panel 4, the silhouette-captain cliffhanger) -- a stylistic ink trick for mood; in a photoreal pipeline a sudden desaturated panel would read as a color-grade bug, not a deliberate reveal.
- Nested, non-standard panel borders bleeding into an unbordered energy backdrop (p3's layered insets floating on a borderless lightning-cracked background) -- a page-layout composition device with no single-panel photoreal equivalent; skip for per-panel generation.
