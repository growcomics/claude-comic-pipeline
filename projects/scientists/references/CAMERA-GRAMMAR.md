# Scientists remake — Camera grammar (from 44-page source analysis + studio extension blocks)

## Source findings (full analysis in spotter report, 2026-07-29)
- Source distribution: ~30% MCU torso-macro, ~20% FULL, ~15% MED, ~15% CU, ~10% INSERT, ~4% ECU, ~4% WIDE.
- Weakness: FULL+torso ≈ 50%; pages 27/35/42 stack 4-6 near-identical fulls; dialogue = flat eye-level MED shot/reverse-shot.
- Growth recipe (keep, it works): ingest insert → face CU → torso MCU → body-part insert → clothing-rip insert → ONE full/cowboy payoff at slight low angle.

## Remake rules (bake into every shotlist)
1. Target ratio ≈ 30 MCU / 25 FULL / 20 MED / 15 CU / 10 INSERT+ECU. Max 2 consecutive FULL panels, then cut to CU/insert/env.
2. FULL body must be EARNED: sequence finale, first reveal of a new size tier, or scale/group panel. Never a mid-beat default.
3. Dialogue: over-the-shoulder both directions, vary distance between reverses; encode power — dominant character from low angle, dominated from high.
4. Use high look-down + top-down for scale/geography beats; dutch only on impact/power-inversion beats.
5. Sell scale with composition (FG/BG size contrast, object interaction like the p16 copy-machine lift), not dialogue claims.
6. Faces: present at start AND payoff of every growth beat; mid-beat stays on torso/part inserts.

## Lighting + staging blocks (adapted from studio/extension/flow-studio-tools content.js — tested wording)
- FRAMING (all panels): staging breaks the camera plane — figures on a diagonal axis, one nearer and larger when two are present, silhouettes slightly overlapping; out-of-focus environmental element grazing a lower corner in extreme foreground; readable environment behind; asymmetric negative space above one shoulder line. No flat front-on eye-level staging unless the beat demands it.
- CAM HEIGHT ("vary per beat" wording): drop below chest height when power/dominance is the story, eye level for connection, above head height for scale/vulnerability/geography — never default low-hero.
- LIGHT (kitchen/morning): low warm morning sun through the window raking almost parallel across the scene, full highlight-to-shadow gradient across every form, ambient-occlusion deepened in creases, subtle warm rim light lifting figures off a half-stop-darker environment, faint dust motes in the sun shafts.
- LIGHT (lab): cool overhead fluorescents as key with a warm practical accent (desk lamp / equipment glow), soft AO in creases, figures a half-stop brighter than the environment, thin cool rim from the glass-door side.
