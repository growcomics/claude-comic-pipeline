# University env reference — DAZ3D look provenance

Per environment-references.md: study the canonical DAZ3D university look, then build a NEW original
Ashgrove courtyard in Flow off it (not a copy), and use DIFFERENT SUB-AREAS per panel.

## DAZ3D university look (research)
Source products studied (visual reference only — not reproduced):
- DAZ3D "FG University" — University Environment Set, MULTIPLE rooms/areas (classrooms, library,
  lounge, open areas), Iray-optimized. The "use different parts of one set" basis.
- DAZ3D "University Lecture Hall" — old European university (~200yr, modernized), arched windows,
  classic architecture, Iray render presets.
- DAZ3D "College Classroom" / "College Dorm" — Iray material/light presets.

### Rendered-look fingerprint to reproduce in Flow
- Iray soft global illumination, realistic stone/wood/foliage PBR materials.
- Old-European campus: honey-coloured stone, arched windows, ivy, colonnade, stone steps, fountain.
- Cinematic golden-hour: warm key + cool rim, long soft shadows, gentle haze, shallow DOF.
- Clean, uncluttered marketing-render staging (simple background requirement).

## Our ORIGINAL location (built in Flow)
**Ashgrove University courtyard** — one consistent campus, framed from different SUB-AREAS per panel:
steps+colonnade, fountain plaza, ivy archway/building corner, autumn-tree lawn, bench/bike-rack edge.
