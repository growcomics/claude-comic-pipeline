# Spin & Swell — 20-panel shotlist (beat sheet)

FMG muscle comic. 4 women (Maria, Roxy, Mei, Linda), one simple DAZ3D living room.
Game: spin the bottle; whoever it points at must FLEX → her muscles surge a tier,
shredding her clothes (but stays covered). Escalates over 4 rounds until all four are
massive and heroic. Fun, hype tone. Tiers per character tracked in [brackets].
Every panel: SIZE · ANGLE · LIGHTING per references/cinematography.md.

---

### Round 0 — Setup
**p01 — WIDE establish · top-down/high 3q · warm bounce, high-key, fairy-light practicals**
The four women sit cross-legged in a circle on a rug, a glass bottle in the middle, snacks
and drinks around, string lights on the wall. Cozy night in. [all tier 1]
- CAPTION: GIRLS' NIGHT. THE RULES WERE SIMPLE.

**p02 — MEDIUM two-shot · eye-level · soft key + warm fill**
Maria leans in, hand on the bottle, grinning, explaining the game. The others listen, amused.
- JADE: SPIN LANDS ON YOU — YOU FLEX. LAST ONE STANDING WINS.
- ROXY: ...THAT'S IT? EASY.

### Round 1 — Roxy
**p03 — ECU bottle · top-down · hard kicker glinting off the glass**
Maria's hand flicks the bottle; it spins fast, motion-blurred on the rug.
- SFX: WHIRRRRR

**p04 — MEDIUM · slight high angle · flat-ish soft light (calm before)**
The bottle slows and stops, neck pointing dead at Roxy. Beat. Roxy's eyebrows shoot up; the
others turn to her with wicked grins.
- ROXY: ...oh no.

**p05 — ECU arms (transformation_beat: arms) · low macro · hard side key + bright rim on the bicep, low fill**
Macro on Roxy's upper arm: the bicep swells and hardens, the crop-top sleeve seam splitting,
threads popping. [roxy → tier 2]
- SFX: FFLEX—

**p06 — FULL solo · LOW hero angle · hard rim/back light carving the muscle, dramatic low-key**
Roxy on her feet, toned and ripped now, one sleeve torn away, throwing a cocky double-bicep,
freckled grin. The other three lean back, wide-eyed. [roxy tier 2]
- ROXY: OKAY, I'M INTO THIS.
- BIANCA: SHOW-OFF.

### Round 2 — Mei
**p07 — MEDIUM · eye-level OTS (over Roxy's shoulder) · warm key + cool rim**
Mei calmly takes the bottle, pushes her glasses up, spins it with one finger.
- MEI: BEAT THAT.

**p08 — ECU bottle · dutch tilt · hard kicker**
The bottle whirls and stops — pointing right back at Mei. Her flat expression cracks.
- MEI: ...of course.
- SFX: tk... tk... tk

**p09 — ECU back/shoulders (transformation_beat: back) · low macro · hard back rim, deep shadow fill**
Macro on Mei's upper back and shoulders flaring wide, the cream blouse ripping straight up the
seam, buttons firing off. [mei → tier 2]
- SFX: RRRRIP!

**p10 — MEDIUM two-shot · low angle · strong rim, low-key dramatic**
Mei flexes broad lats and delts, blouse in tatters but covering, glasses askew, a slow smug
smile. Roxy points, delighted. [mei tier 2, roxy tier 2]
- ROXY: NOW WE'RE TALKING.
- MEI: STATISTICALLY, I WAS DUE.

### Round 3 — Linda
**p11 — WIDE group · high 3q · bright high-key, warm bounce**
All four half into it now, two already buff. Linda grabs the bottle dramatically, hair flipping,
and spins it hard.
- BIANCA: MY TURN. WATCH AND LEARN.

**p12 — ECU bottle · top-down · glinting kicker**
The bottle screams around and stops, neck aimed straight at Linda. She gasps with delight.
- BIANCA: FINALLY. ME.

**p13 — ECU chest/shoulders (transformation_beat: chest) · low macro · hard side key, bright rim, low fill**
Macro on Linda's shoulders and upper chest swelling powerfully, the off-shoulder top straining
and splitting at the seams, gold hoops swinging. [bianca → tier 3 — she over-commits]
- SFX: FWOOMP—

**p14 — FULL solo · LOW hero angle · rim/back light + warm key, dramatic**
Linda strikes a fierce side-chest pose, now the biggest yet, top torn but covered, curls wild,
triumphant. The others gawk. [bianca tier 3]
- BIANCA: BEAT. THAT.

### Round 4 — Maria (the closer)
**p15 — MEDIUM · eye-level · confident warm key + cool rim**
Maria cracks her knuckles, unimpressed, and spins the bottle with authority.
- JADE: AMATEURS.

**p16 — ECU bottle → MCU Maria · low angle · hard kicker, tense low-key**
The bottle stops on Maria. Slow grin spreads across her face; the others brace.
- JADE: NOW YOU'RE IN TROUBLE.

**p17 — FULL (transformation_beat: suit_fail) · low macro/full · hard rim everywhere, explosive low-key**
Maria EXPLODES into muscle — the biggest surge of all — tank top and leggings shredding at every
seam at once, the room lights flaring. [jade → tier 4] The others shield their eyes.
- SFX: KRRRAKOOOM!

**p18 — FULL solo splash · WORM'S-EYE hero low · monumental back rim + warm key, epic low-key**
Maria towers, fully massive and heroic, tank in tatters but covered, throwing a colossal
double-biceps pose, ponytail whipping. Pure power. [jade tier 4]
- JADE: AND THAT'S A FLEX.

### Finale
**p19 — WIDE group hero shot · LOW angle · big rim on all four, warm high-key, room a happy mess**
All four enormous and ripped, clothes torn (covered), striking a group flex together, laughing,
snacks scattered, a couch cushion knocked over. [jade 4, bianca 3, roxy 2, mei 2]
- ALL (shared): BEST. GAME. EVER.

**p20 — MEDIUM finale gag · eye-level · soft warm key, cozy bounce**
The four flop together on the couch, exhausted and grinning, the little bottle on the table between
them. One eyes it.
- ROXY: ...SAME TIME NEXT WEEK?
- CAPTION: REMATCH PENDING.
